Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the website amanpaaji-analyst.github.io (the personal portfolio of Aman Kumar) collects when you log in, and how it is used.

What is collected

  • Your email address.
  • If you sign up with a password: a password stored only as a salted, one-way hash. The password itself is never stored or shown.
  • If you use Google login: the verified email address that Google provides. No other Google data is requested.
  • The date and time of your first and latest login, how many times you logged in, and the login method (Google or email and password).

Why it is collected

To give access to the portfolio, to keep a record of visitors, and to let the owner know when a new visitor logs in.

Who can see it

Only the site owner can see visitor records. The data is stored on Cloudflare services. It is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Storage in your browser

The site remembers in your browser tab that you have logged in, so you are not asked again until you open a new session. No advertising cookies are used.
